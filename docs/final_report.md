# Final Report Structure & Validation Metrics

The validation report generated by the **AI Startup Idea Validator with Market Analysis Assistance** system evaluates startup concepts across an **8-dimension deterministic scoring rubric**:

---

## 1. 8-Dimension Deterministic Scoring Rubric

The overall viability score is calculated on an objective 100-point scale across 8 weighted dimensions:

| Dimension | Field Name | Max Points | Weight | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Market Opportunity** | `market_opportunity_score` | 20 | 20% | TAM, SAM, SOM sizing ($B) and projected sector CAGR % |
| **Innovation & Differentiation** | `innovation_score` | 15 | 15% | Novelty of value proposition, AI automation depth, and keyword differentiation |
| **Competition & Moat** | `competition_score` | 15 | 15% | Incumbent density and defensive moat strength |
| **Scalability Potential** | `scalability_score` | 15 | 15% | Software margin profile, expandability, and recurring revenue mechanics |
| **Technical Feasibility** | `technical_feasibility_score` | 10 | 10% | Feasibility of 4-week MVP, stack maturity, engineering complexity |
| **Revenue Model Viability** | `revenue_model_score` | 10 | 10% | Monetization mechanics (B2B SaaS, usage) and willingness to pay |
| **Execution & Risk Resilience** | `execution_risk_score` | 10 | 10% | Inverse financial, technical, and regulatory risk severities |
| **Market Timing** | `market_timing_score` | 5 | 5% | Industry tailwinds, macro trends, and catalytic timing |
| **Total Viability Index** | `overall_viability_score` | **100** | **100%** | **Mathematically computed sum of all 8 dimension scores** |

---

## 2. Strategic Verdict Categories

The overall viability index is mapped to clear, standardized strategic recommendations:

- **`PROCEED`** (&ge; 78 / 100): High market opportunity with manageable risks, clear differentiation, and strong MVP viability. Recommended for rapid prototype execution and founder validation.
- **`CAUTION`** (65 &ndash; 77 / 100): Promising core concept with notable headwinds or unverified market/competitor evidence. Requires targeted de-risking and direct customer discovery prior to heavy engineering or capital commitment.
- **`PIVOT`** (50 &ndash; 64 / 100): Substantial execution, competitive, or structural friction identified. Refine target audience, differentiation, or business model before proceeding.
- **`STOP`** (&lt; 50 / 100): High structural friction, minimal addressable demand, or entrenched incumbent dominance. Re-evaluate core problem statement.

---

## 3. Supplementary Investor & Decision Metrics

- **Investor Readiness Score** (0–100): Diligence readiness for seed/angel fundraising.
- **Funding Probability** (0–100%): Projected likelihood of attracting venture backing.
- **Product-Market Fit (PMF) Index** (0–100): Urgency of customer pain points relative to solution value proposition.
- **Overall Confidence Score** (20–95%): Evidence completeness metric (capped at 65% when live web research is unavailable).
